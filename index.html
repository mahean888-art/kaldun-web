      03 · Mission Foresight infrastructure
      F·M

      Make foresight infrastructure for the financial and physical world.

      There’s forecasting, and there’s foresight.

      Forecasting anticipates the conditions ahead, and machines now match or beat the best human forecasters.

      But every commitment sits inside a system of other institutions, agreements, and rules, and that system has outrun the tools we plan with.

      Today those systems are modeled on spreadsheets, one scenario at a time.

      A Foresight Machine runs the whole system forward, then intervenes: a different allocation, new terms, or a sequence that makes a project possible.

      No one can experiment on a real institution. Its team can experiment on its Foresight Machine, before anything is committed.

      05 · Validation The Record

      A Foresight Machine earns trust one way: by its record.

      Every machine is tested where it can fail safely, then where it cannot cheat, then where it counts.

      1. 01RL environments

        Where it can fail safely.

        Its own world, a task, and an answer that can be checked exactly. Every plan meets an adversary searching for the smallest shock that breaks it.

        No assumed probabilities · adversarial search

      2. 02Replay

        Where it cannot cheat.

        History it has never seen, scored on what actually happened.

        Held-out history · zero lookahead

      3. 03Live decisions

        Where it counts.

        The institution’s real decisions, recorded before the outcome and scored after.

        Recorded before the outcome · scored after

      1. 01

        Built from evidence. Every rule in the model links to its source.

      2. 02

        Tested against what it replaces. Every answer is compared with the team’s own plan, under the same rules.

      3. 03

        Recorded before the fact. Every answer is written down before the outcome is known.

      4. 04

        Corrected by outcomes. Every miss becomes a new environment. A change ships only if it does better on unseen history and worse on no past case.

      Sovereignty

      The world is shared. Each institution’s machine, its contracts, its records, and what it learns stay strictly its own.
