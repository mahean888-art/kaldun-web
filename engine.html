Resolution

Every forecast begins
with a rule for judging it.

A resolution contract names the source and the rule before the forecast is published: which register, which database, which capture, and who adjudicates a dispute. When the date arrives, resolution is a lookup, not an argument.

A model that never resolves
never learns whether it was right.
