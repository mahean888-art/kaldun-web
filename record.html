Commitment

The record cannot be
rewritten after reality arrives.

Selected public and customer-authorized forecasts are cryptographically committed before the outcome is known. The commitment proves that the original forecast, odds, information cutoff, model version, and resolution rules were not rewritten after the fact.

Integrity by commitment.
Accuracy by resolution.

Cryptography proves integrity. It does not prove accuracy. The score does.

What the commitment is for

Prove that the forecast existed before the outcome.

Prevent the original probabilities from being silently changed.

Preserve every update as a new version.

Allow independent verification of the forecast record.

Prevent Kaldun from presenting only successful forecasts after resolution.
