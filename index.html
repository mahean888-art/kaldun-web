Mission

Find a way to every future worth building.

A Foresight Machine is a working model of the world around a commitment. Run it forward to see what could happen. Ask it what it would take.

There’s forecasting, and there’s foresight.

Forecasting anticipates the conditions ahead, and machines now do it about as well as the best people.

But every commitment sits inside a system larger than the decision itself: other participants, agreements, constraints, and the conditions they meet. The outcome turns on how they move together.

Today those systems are modeled on spreadsheets, one scenario at a time.

Foresight runs the whole system forward, then searches for a better arrangement: a different allocation, new terms, a sequence that makes a project possible.

Models like these took specialists months to build. AI is making them fast to build and practical to keep current.

Domains

Wherever capital is committed.

Every domain gets the same three questions: what happens if conditions change, what changes if we act differently, and what arrangement could make this work.

  1. Reserves & liquidity

    Issuers, networks and funds that owe cash on demand

    1. What if partner balances grow 40% next year?
    2. Which bank should carry our weekend redemptions?
    3. What would let us redeem instantly, any hour?

  2. Credit & collateral

    Lenders and managers whose collateral moves with rates and prices

    1. What if rates stay higher for two more years?
    2. Which collateral should we post, and where?
    3. What terms let us lend where we decline today?

  3. Risk & insurance

    Insurers writing new risks with little history: cyber, AI, digital assets, climate

    1. What would one provider’s outage cost across our book?
    2. Where should the trigger sit, and how should cover be layered?
    3. Could we cover a risk no one will write today?

  4. Projects & supply chains

    Developers and manufacturers whose plans depend on delivery

    1. What if equipment arrives six months late?
    2. More inventory, or a second supplier?
    3. What makes this financeable at today’s rates?

Method

A model to explore with. A record to improve from.

Trust

Models earn trust against reality.

Every recommendation shows its sources, its assumptions, and how confident the model is.

  1. 01

    Built from evidence.

    Every rule traces to a record, a contract or an observation.

  2. 02

    Tested against alternatives.

    Every recommendation is compared with current practice, under the same rules.

  3. 03

    Kept current.

    The model changes as the institution does, and every version is kept.

  4. 04

    Corrected by outcomes.

    Every expectation is recorded before the fact, and every miss improves the model.
