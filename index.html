Sec-02 · Foresight02

There’s forecasting, and there’s foresight.

Forecasting anticipates the conditions ahead, and machines now do it about as well as the best people.

But every commitment sits inside a system larger than the decision itself: other participants, agreements, constraints, and the conditions they meet. The outcome turns on how they move together.

Foresight runs that system forward, then searches for a better arrangement: a different allocation, new terms, a sequence that makes a project possible.

Models like these took specialists months to build. AI is making them fast to build and practical to keep current.

Foresight Machines is building AI to expand the choices institutions can make, and the futures those choices make possible.

Sec-03 · Questions03

Three questions before every commitment.

Ask what could happen, compare what you could do, or start from the outcome you need.

01Conditions

What happens if conditions change?

Multi-brand digital-dollar issuer

If our largest reserve bank failed, could we still meet every redemption on time?

The plan

Reserves sit across four banks, none above the concentration limit.

The simulation

Two of the four route payments through the same correspondent. Lose it, and more than half the cash freezes at once.

What it finds

The concentration is in the plumbing, not the balances.

02Choices

What changes if we act differently?

Bank treasury

Hold a larger liquid-asset buffer, or secure a committed funding line?

The plan

Grow the buffer by a fifth.

The simulation

In the stresses that matter, the buffer is large enough but slow to turn into cash. A committed line closes the gap at lower carrying cost.

What it finds

A smaller buffer, plus a committed line whose draw is tested regularly.

03Arrangements

What arrangement could make this work?

Digital-dollar issuer

What would let us offer same-day redemption to institutional holders?

The plan

Same-day redemption only below a fixed size.

The simulation

Large redemptions cluster in stressed markets, exactly when Treasury bills are slowest to turn into cash.

What it finds

A standing repo agreement that turns bills into cash within the day, sized to the largest cluster tested.

RequiresA repo counterparty and an operating test.

01Conditions

What happens if conditions change?

Private credit fund

What if rates stay high for two more years?

The plan

Borrowers refinance as rates fall.

The simulation

Without refinancing, covenant breaches begin in one sector within eighteen months.

What it finds

The risk is one sector’s refinancing wall, not the portfolio average.

02Choices

What changes if we act differently?

Asset manager

When margin calls spike, sell bonds or borrow against them?

The plan

Sell bonds when cash runs short.

The simulation

In a rate shock, calls peak just as bond prices fall, so forced sales lock in the losses.

What it finds

Borrow against the bonds through repo, and sell only once the calls subside.

03Arrangements

What arrangement could make this work?

Equipment lender

What terms would let us lend to operators we decline today?

The plan

Decline fleet operators with less than two years of history.

The simulation

Losses track the equipment’s resale value far more than the borrower’s age.

What it finds

Lend against the equipment with a manufacturer buy-back, and raise the advance rate as payment history builds.

RequiresThe manufacturer’s buy-back agreement.

01Conditions

What happens if conditions change?

Regional property insurer

What if two storms hit in the same season?

The plan

Reinsurance covers the largest single event.

The simulation

The second storm lands after the limit is spent, just as the renewal is priced.

What it finds

The exposure is the second event, not the larger one.

02Choices

What changes if we act differently?

Regional property insurer

Buy a higher limit, or hold more capital?

The plan

Raise the reinsurance limit.

The simulation

A reinstatement provision costs less, and covers the two-storm case the higher limit misses.

What it finds

Keep the limit. Buy the reinstatement.

03Arrangements

What arrangement could make this work?

Specialty insurer

Could we cover coastal homes we decline today?

The plan

Decline every home within a mile of the coast.

The simulation

Losses concentrate in homes without roof retrofits.

What it finds

Offer cover on condition of a retrofit, with a premium credit for it.

RequiresAn inspection partner.

01Conditions

What happens if conditions change?

Solar-and-storage developer

What if the transformers arrive six months late?

The plan

Operations start before the first debt payment.

The simulation

Revenue slips past the first payment, and the storage contract’s delay penalty starts the same month.

What it finds

The binding constraint is the penalty clause, not the loan.

02Choices

What changes if we act differently?

Electronics manufacturer

Hold more inventory, or qualify a second supplier?

The plan

Double the safety stock.

The simulation

A second source for one controller chip covers most of the exposure, at a fraction of the carrying cost.

What it finds

Qualify a second source for that one part.

03Arrangements

What arrangement could make this work?

Infrastructure developer

What would make this project financeable at today’s rates?

The plan

One construction loan, sized for the full build.

The simulation

Debt coverage fails in the high-rate cases.

What it finds

Draw the loan in stages with equipment deliveries, and pre-sell storage capacity to the utility.

RequiresThe utility’s agreement.

Sec-05 · Trust05

Models earn trust against reality.

Every recommendation shows its sources, its assumptions, and how confident the model is.

  1. 01

    Built from evidence.

    Rules and relationships trace to operating records, contracts and observed behavior. Missing evidence is marked, not filled in.

  2. 02

    Tested against alternatives.

    Each recommendation is compared with current practice and strong conventional methods under the same objectives and constraints, including the conditions under which it fails.

  3. 03

    Kept current.

    The model is updated as contracts, counterparties and conditions change. Every version is archived, so any past recommendation can be reproduced.

  4. 04↺ 01

    Corrected by outcomes.

    Expectations are recorded before events unfold. Misses become proposed revisions, and a revision must improve results on separate cases before it is used.
