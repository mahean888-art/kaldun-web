Mission

Build foresight infrastructure for the financial and physical world.

There’s forecasting, and there’s foresight.

Forecasting tells you what is likely. Machines now do it as well as the best people.

But no commitment is settled alone. Other institutions, agreements and rules decide it, and that system has outrun the tools we plan with.

Inside most institutions it still lives in a spreadsheet, one scenario at a time.

A Foresight Machine runs the whole system forward, finds where the plan breaks, then intervenes: a different allocation, new terms, a sequence that makes a project possible.

No one can experiment on a real institution.

Anyone can experiment on its Foresight Machine, before anything is committed.

Validation

A Foresight Machine earns trust the way a trader does: by its record.

Every machine is tested where it can fail safely, then where it cannot cheat, then where it counts.

  1. 01RL environments

    Where it can fail safely.

    Its own world, a task and an answer that can be checked exactly. Every plan meets an adversary searching for the smallest shock that breaks it.

  2. 02Replay

    Where it cannot cheat.

    History it has never seen, scored on what actually happened.

  3. 03Live decisions

    Where it counts.

    The institution’s real decisions, recorded before the outcome and scored after.

The four rules

  1. No. 01Built from evidence.Every rule in the model links to its source.
  2. No. 02Tested against what it replaces.Every answer is compared with the team’s own plan, under the same rules.
  3. No. 03Recorded before the fact.Every answer is written down before the outcome is known.
  4. No. 04Corrected by outcomes.Every miss becomes a new environment. A change ships only if it does better on unseen history and worse on no past case.

The world is shared. Each institution’s machine, its records and what it learns stay its own.
