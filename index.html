Every institution will have a Foresight Machine to simulate its world and discover what works.

A Foresight Machine models an institution and the world it depends on: its capital, contracts, counterparties, markets, and rules. It simulates every major decision before it is made, discovers better arrangements, and learns from each outcome.

How it works

How a Foresight Machine reaches an answer.

Mission

Make foresight infrastructure for the financial and physical world.

There’s forecasting, and there’s foresight.

Forecasting anticipates the conditions ahead, and machines now match or beat the best human forecasters.

But every commitment sits inside a system larger than the decision itself: other participants, agreements, constraints, and the conditions they meet. The outcome turns on how they move together.

Today those systems are modeled on spreadsheets, one scenario at a time.

Foresight runs the whole system forward, then searches for a better arrangement: a different allocation, new terms, a sequence that makes a project possible.

No one can experiment on a real institution.

Anyone can experiment on its Foresight Machine, before anything is committed.

Worlds

Wherever capital is committed.

Each is a world of its own, with its institutions, its flows and its rules, joined to the others by the money that moves between them. We model the world of money first, then follow it into the worlds it builds.

01 / 04

Money, payments and credit

Digital dollars and instant rails run around the clock on top of banks that keep business hours, so cash sits idle and partner payouts are set bluntly.

Who decides
Issuers, banks, payment companies, exchanges, lenders and funds.
What it adds to the world
Mint and redemption waves through the day, bank cutoffs, rate paths, tokenized collateral and funds, and partner terms tied to how long balances stay.
Optimize
Where should liquidity sit so every redemption and payout clears, at any hour, with the least cash trapped?
Discover
What would it take to redeem instantly for a new partner?
Coordinate
Which distribution terms grow balances that stay, for us and the exchange?
Underwrite
What should it cost to cover redemptions that cannot clear over a weekend?

02 / 04

Commodities and global trade

Cargo in transit, futures margin calls and supplier credit sit in separate spreadsheets, behind a $2.5 trillion trade-finance gap.

Who decides
Commodity traders, trade and supply-chain financiers, producers and buyers.
What it adds to the world
Port and route delays tied to inventory hedges, letters of credit and the buyer’s balance sheet.
Optimize
Hedge now, or hold and finance the inventory?
Discover
What would it take to finance suppliers we decline today?
Coordinate
Which supplier finance terms work for the buyer, the supplier and the bank?
Underwrite
What should cover for a cargo held at port cost, given everything else on the route?

03 / 04

Insurance and emergent risk

AI liability, cyber, autonomous systems, digital assets and a changing grid and climate lack long loss histories, behind a protection gap above $420 billion.

Who decides
Insurers, reinsurers and underwriting agencies.
What it adds to the world
How failures happen and spread, accumulation across a book, parametric triggers and reinsurance layers.
Optimize
Where should this layer attach?
Discover
What would it take to cover a risk no one writes today?
Coordinate
Which cover makes this project financeable for both the lender and the insurer?
Underwrite
How should a risk with no loss history be priced and layered?

04 / 04

Energy, compute and megainfrastructure

Grids, fabs and gigawatt data centers stall when equipment lead times collide with rigid project-finance terms.

Who decides
Developers, operators and project financiers.
What it adds to the world
Transformer and interconnection schedules tied to power purchase terms, staged capital draws and debt service.
Optimize
Stage the build, or finance it whole?
Discover
What would it take to finance this project at today’s rates?
Coordinate
Which offtake and financing terms hold for the developer, the buyer and the lender?
Underwrite
What should cover for a late project cost, when equipment, power and financing depend on each other?

Validation

Every answer is written down before the outcome, then scored.

The world is shared. Each institution’s machine, records and what it learns stay its own, and every outcome makes the next model better. A change to the model is kept only if it does better on past cases and worse on none.

  1. 01

    Every rule in the model points to a contract, record or observation.

  2. 02

    Every answer is scored against the rules, held-out history and stressed regimes, and compared with what the team does today.

  3. 03

    Every expectation is timestamped, cryptographically hashed and committed before the outcome.

  4. 04

    Each machine is trained on its own institution’s tasks and context, and rewarded only when its answers hold against what happens.
