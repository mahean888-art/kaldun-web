Every institution will have a Foresight Machine to simulate its world and discover what could work.

A Foresight Machine models an institution and the world it depends on: its capital, contracts, counterparties, markets, and rules. It simulates every major decision before it is made, discovers better arrangements, and learns from each outcome.

Mission

Make foresight infrastructure for the financial and physical world.

There’s forecasting, and there’s foresight.

Forecasting anticipates the conditions ahead, and machines now do it about as well as the best people.

But every commitment sits inside a system larger than the decision itself: other participants, agreements, constraints, and the conditions they meet. The outcome turns on how they move together.

Today those systems are modeled on spreadsheets, one scenario at a time.

Foresight runs the whole system forward, then searches for a better arrangement: a different allocation, new terms, a sequence that makes a project possible.

No one can experiment on a real institution.

Anyone can experiment on its Foresight Machine, thousands of times, before anything is committed.

Where we start

Every digital dollar is a promise.

To pay anyone, at any hour, through any partner. That promise runs through a world of issuers, banks, payment companies, exchanges, lenders and funds, each depending on the others. We model that world once. Each institution in it gets its own Foresight Machine.

Worlds

Wherever capital is committed.

Each is a world of its own, with its institutions, its flows and its rules, joined to the others by the money that moves between them. We model the world of money first, then follow it into the worlds it builds.

01 / 04

Programmable money

Digital dollars, tokenized deposits and funds, payments, lending and collateral.

Who decides
Issuers, banks, payment companies, exchanges, lenders and funds.
What it adds to the world
Accounts, rails, counterparties, collateral, rates.
Optimize
How much cash should we keep at each bank this week?
Discover
What would it take to redeem instantly for a new partner?
Coordinate
Which distribution terms grow balances that stay, for us and the exchange?

02 / 04

Trade and commodities

Goods that are financed, hedged, stored and moved, from raw materials to the supply chains behind the buildout.

Who decides
Commodity traders, trade and supply-chain financiers, producers and buyers.
What it adds to the world
Inventory, shipping, storage, hedges.
Optimize
Hedge now, or hold and finance the inventory?
Discover
What would it take to finance suppliers we decline today?
Coordinate
Which supplier finance terms work for the buyer, the supplier and the bank?

03 / 04

Insurance and risk transfer

New and emergent risks with little history: AI, cyber, digital assets, a changing climate.

Who decides
Insurers, reinsurers and underwriting agencies.
What it adds to the world
Losses, triggers, layers, accumulation across a book.
Optimize
Where should this layer attach?
Discover
What would it take to cover a risk no one writes today?
Coordinate
Which cover makes this project financeable for both the lender and the insurer?

04 / 04

Energy and infrastructure

Power, grids, fabs and data centers.

Who decides
Developers, operators and project financiers.
What it adds to the world
Construction, delivery, capacity, long horizons.
Optimize
Stage the build, or finance it whole?
Discover
What would it take to finance this project at today’s rates?
Coordinate
Which offtake and financing terms hold for the developer, the buyer and the lender?

Method

How a Foresight Machine reaches an answer.

Validation

Every answer is written down before the outcome, then scored.

The world is shared. Each institution’s machine, records and what it learns stay its own, and every outcome makes the next model better.

  1. 01

    Every rule in the model points to a contract, record or observation.

  2. 02

    Every answer is scored against the rules, the past and a thousand futures, and compared with what the team does today.

  3. 03

    Every expectation is timestamped before the outcome.

  4. 04

    A model change is kept only if it does better on past cases and worse on none.
