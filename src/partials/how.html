04 / RECORD

Committed before the outcome

The answer, its evidence and the team’s own plan are hashed and timestamped together before the outcome is known, then scored when it arrives. Every miss becomes a new test.

HashTimestampScoreLearn

Sealed before the fact

Each record carries the hash of the one before it, so no answer can be changed once the outcome is known.
