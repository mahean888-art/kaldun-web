The Record

Made before
the answer was known.

The Record is the machine’s public memory — a permanent, auditable account of every claim it has made.

Committed before the outcome

Every claim the machine commits is sealed under a cryptographic hash and timestamped before the world answers. It cannot be quietly edited.

Calibrated, not confident

When the machine says 70%, about 70% of those events happen. That is the bar, and it is measurable.

Scored by reality

When the outcome arrives, the score posts beside the claim. Anyone can check.

Trained on the difference

The gap between what was believed and what happened is the machine’s next lesson.

A decision should not disappear after it is made.

Research residency

1654

Systems that learn from time.

In 1654, Pascal and Fermat traded letters about dividing the stakes of an unfinished game — and began the mathematics of probability. The unfinished game is now the world.

Project 1654 is our residency for superforecasters, builders and researchers — anyone advancing the science of predicting future states. Fellows get a salary, compute, and the live state and the Record as experimental infrastructure — and every project seals at least one claim.

Nothing at Foresight Machines goes ungraded.
