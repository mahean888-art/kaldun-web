Sec-01Foresight

There’s forecasting, and there’s foresight.

Forecasting anticipates the conditions ahead, and machines now do it about as well as the best people.

But every commitment depends on a system larger than the decision itself: other participants, agreements, constraints, and the conditions they meet. What happens depends on how they move together.

Foresight runs that system forward, then searches for a better arrangement: a different allocation, new terms, a sequence that makes a project possible.

Building models like these used to take months of specialist work. AI is changing the economics of that work.

Foresight Machines is building AI to expand the choices institutions can make, and the futures those choices make possible.

Sec-02Three questions

From what could happen to what could work.

Three questions a model of your system can answer.

01What happens if conditions change?Follow a change through the system to see which dependencies and commitments it affects.

02What changes if we act differently?Compare allocations, terms and timing to understand their costs and consequences.

03What arrangement could make this work?Search for combinations the original plan did not consider, and examine what they require.

Sec-04Evidence

Models earn trust against reality.

Our standard is evidence: traceable assumptions, comparisons with real alternatives, and a record of what happened after the decision.

  1. 01Ground the model. Connect its rules and relationships to operating records, contracts and observed behavior. Make uncertainty and missing evidence visible.
  2. 02Challenge the result. Compare alternatives under the same objectives and constraints. Investigate the conditions under which a recommendation changes or fails.
  3. 03Improve from outcomes. Record expectations before events unfold. Use the resulting errors to propose revisions, then test those revisions on separate cases.
